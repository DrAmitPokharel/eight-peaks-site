What We Do

Engineering support at every stage

We work with clients to understand the problem, evaluate options, and deliver sound, well-documented engineering work — from broad consulting and project delivery down to specific design disciplines. A few examples of the work we take on:

Structural Design

Code-compliant structural design and analysis for buildings and small structures, from concept through construction documents.

Residential Floor Plan Design

Floor plans and layouts for new homes, additions, and remodels, developed with construction and permitting in mind.

3D Rendering

Visual models of a design concept to support client review, stakeholder buy-in, and permitting submittals.

Finite Element Modeling

Detailed FEM/FEA analysis of structural components and systems under real-world loading conditions.

Sign & Wind Load Engineering

Stamped structural calculations and footing design for pole, monument, and wall signs to support permit approval.

Metal Building Foundation & Anchorage

Site-specific foundation and anchor bolt design for pre-engineered metal buildings and small commercial shells.

Construction Drawings & Drafting

Clear, permit-ready construction documents and detailing prepared in AutoCAD and Civil 3D.

Consulting

Independent, straightforward advice to help clients make informed decisions with confidence.

Project Management

Coordinated, well-documented delivery that keeps projects on schedule and within scope.

Foundation & Structural Inspection

Condition assessment of existing building foundations and structural systems, with clear, documented findings.

These are examples, not the full list — if your project doesn't fit neatly into a category above, get in touch and let's talk about it.
