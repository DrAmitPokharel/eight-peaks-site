About Us

Built on a simple idea: do the engineering right.

Eight Peaks Engineering, PLLC provides professional engineering services with an emphasis on technical rigor, practical solutions, and honest communication.

Our Approach

Straightforward engineering, clearly explained

Every project starts with understanding the real problem — not just the stated one. We evaluate options on their technical merits, document our reasoning clearly, and stay available to answer questions throughout a project's life, not just at kickoff.

Whether the engagement is a single design task or ongoing project support, our goal is the same: work you can rely on and a process you can follow.

Leadership

Meet the CEO

Dr. Amit Pokharel, PhD, PE, PACP — Founder & CEO

PhD, Civil Engineering Licensed PE (Texas) NASSCO PACP / LACP / MACP

Dr. Pokharel is a licensed Professional Engineer with nearly a decade of combined industry and research leadership in civil and structural engineering. He holds a PhD in Civil Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington, where his doctoral research applied machine learning to service-life prediction for underground infrastructure — work that now informs his approach to practical, data-driven engineering.

His background spans finite element analysis of buried pipe and structural systems, multi-sensor infrastructure inspection, and project management, including leading a 30-member team through the repair and rehabilitation of 24 earthquake-damaged highway bridges in Nepal. He founded Eight Peaks Engineering to bring that same combination of technical rigor and hands-on delivery to clients directly.

  • Licensed Professional Engineer — Texas Board of Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors
  • Research leadership on infrastructure inspection projects for municipal and state agencies across Texas and Oklahoma
  • Charter President, Dallas Metro Professional Lions Club
